01 / CAMPAIGN SPOTLIGHT

A giveaway that sparked conversation.

For THK Glow, we ran a skincare giveaway designed to encourage participation, comments and discovery. The campaign helped bring in approximately 1,000 new followers according to our campaign results, while generating substantial engagement and reach.

CAMPAIGN CONCEPT

Choose your dream five.

The campaign invited participants to choose five skincare products from a curated selection. The interactive prompt encouraged product discussion and sharing, rather than presenting a static advertisement.

Campaign content included promotional stories, a giveaway reel, and audience comments.
