HIFZA HANIF — CREATIVE MARKETING PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the complete website folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in a browser.
3. Keep index.html, taara.html, style.css, script.js, the images and videos folders,
   and Hifza_Hanif_CV.pdf together inside the same folder.

BRAND LOGOS
The supplied logo images have been saved locally in images/brands/ for:
JOIR Store, Zarafae, VitaMind (used on the Vitamin D Pakistan card),
THK Glow, Paper Dope, Jamoka Properties, Padel Club, AETEA Sparkling Tea,
Big Brands AE, and Aesthetic & Dental Avenue.
The existing Taara logo is under images/taara/.
Aesthetic & Dental Avenue also has a supplied logo saved locally as images/brands/aesthetic-dental-avenue.png. Its source was a screenshot; the phone interface was cropped out.

TAARA SELECTED WORK
Click the Taara card to open taara.html for sample creatives, reel posters,
and 3 locally playable MP4 videos. The displayed work is a selection, not the
entire creative portfolio.

ADDING MORE LOGOS
Add each new logo image to images/brands/ and update that brand card in
index.html, preserving its existing Instagram link. The brand image styling
is in style.css under 'Supplied original brand logos'.

TECHNICAL
HTML with external CSS (style.css) and lightweight JS (script.js).
The logo and Taara media files are local and do not require internet access.
Instagram, THK Glow, and Coursera verification links do require internet.
